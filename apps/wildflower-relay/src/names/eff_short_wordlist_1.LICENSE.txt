eff_short_wordlist_1.txt is the EFF Short Wordlist 1 by the Electronic
Frontier Foundation, unmodified, as published at
https://www.eff.org/files/2016/09/08/eff_short_wordlist_1.txt
(see https://www.eff.org/dice).

The relay draws default tunnel names from it and skips the one word that is
not plain lowercase letters (`yo-yo`), so every name is exactly two words
joined by one hyphen.
